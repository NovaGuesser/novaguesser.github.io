Where is this on campus?


AI Use Disclamer

I made a prototype of this page using no AI and an HTML tutorial by freeCodeCamp.org on Youtube. Afterwards, I used VS Code's AI sidebar to give the page a topbar, a better menu system for all the campus locations, the popup windows, and the buttons that open the popups. I also used Claude to get the site looking nice on mobile. The logos and pictures are all made by me.

How to Play

🟩 Guess the building that this photo is either taken within, or taken closest to!

🟨 Yellow guesses mean you are on the correct sub-campus. (For example, Cabrini Campus or West Campus)

🟥 The answer will NOT be inside of a resident hall.

⬛ You get 6 guesses to find the right location.

Good Guess!
